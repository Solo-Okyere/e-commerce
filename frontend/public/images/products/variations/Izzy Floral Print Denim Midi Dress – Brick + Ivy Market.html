The Izzy Floral Print Denim Midi Dress brings garden-fresh beauty to the durability of denim. A vibrant floral print transforms classic denim into something unexpectedly romantic, while the midi length offers flattering coverage that works from day to night. The structured denim fabric provides shape and support, creating a polished silhouette that holds its own. It's the perfect marriage of casual and feminine – a dress that feels special without trying too hard.

Styling Tip:

Pair the Izzy with white sneakers and a crossbody bag for effortless daytime style, or dress it up with heeled sandals and statement earrings for dinner or date night. The floral print pairs beautifully with denim jackets for tonal layering, or try a solid cardigan in a color pulled from the print. Add a wide belt to cinch the waist and define your shape, or keep it relaxed and flowing for bohemian ease. Ankle boots and a leather jacket give it edgy appeal, while espadrilles and a straw hat lean into the romantic floral vibe. Let the print be the star – keep accessories simple with delicate jewelry and solid-colored bags.

HOW WE MEASURE OUR ITEMS

TOPS:

  • Bust is measured 1 inch down from the armpit seam. Then measured across, doubling the measurement for a full bust size.
  • Length is measured from the middle of the shoulder down to the bottom of the top.
  • Arm length is measured at edge of shoulder down to bottom of sleeve.

PANTS:

  • Length is measured from the middle of the waist down to the bottom of the pant leg.
  • Rise is measured from inseam up to the waist
  • Waist is measured across, doubling for an accurate waist measurement.
  • Inseam is measured from top of inseam down to the edge of the pant leg

DRESSES:

  • Bust is measured 1 inch down from the armpit seam. Then measured across, doubling the measurement for a full bust size.
  • Length is measured from the middle of the shoulder down to the bottom of the dress.
  • Arm length is measured at edge of shoulder down to bottom of sleeve

JUMPSUITS:

  • Bust is measured 1 inch down from the armpit seam. Then measured across, doubling the measurement for a full bust size.
  • Length is measured from the middle of the shoulder down to the bottom of the pant leg.
  • Rise is measured from inseam up to the waist
  • Arm length is measured at edge of shoulder down to bottom of sleeve

SKIRTS:

  • Waist is measured across, doubling for an accurate waist measurement.
  • Length is measured from the middle of the waist down to the bottom of the skirt.
